# Condenser replacement field guide photo list

This list supplies the P01-P20 photo references in the TurtlePen condenser
replacement workflow. The images are intended to be generated or staged on
de-energized training equipment, then reviewed by a qualified HVAC technician
before publication.

## Shared generation direction

Prefix every image prompt with:

> Documentary instructional photograph for a professional HVAC apprentice
> field guide. Realistic residential split-system outdoor condenser and service
> tools, neutral unbranded equipment, clean natural color, even work lighting,
> technically plausible connections, landscape 4:3 framing, high detail where
> the procedure is being shown, no dramatic effects, no marketing composition,
> no visible company logos, no legible personal information.

Apply these constraints to every image:

- Show a trained adult technician using appropriate task-specific PPE.
- Stage electrical-cabinet images on locked-out, verified de-energized training
  equipment. Never depict hands contacting exposed energized conductors.
- Do not invent readable voltage, pressure, vacuum, charge, torque, clearance,
  or refrigerant values. Instrument screens may be blank, obscured, or carry a
  clearly fictional `TRAINING` display for later annotation.
- Do not imply that a photograph alone proves a safety condition. The caption
  must identify the verification or documentation the technician performs.
- Keep ports, hoses, valves, cylinders, meters, and conductors physically
  plausible. Reject images with merged tools, impossible hose routing, missing
  PPE, open flames near uncontrolled refrigerant, or unsafe cylinder handling.
- Leave clean negative space along the lower edge for a caption and photo ID.

## Review checklist

Before any image enters the guide, verify:

1. The depicted configuration is physically possible and matches the caption.
2. Safety controls are visible where the image could otherwise normalize a
   hazardous action.
3. No generated number could be mistaken for a required field value.
4. Equipment, refrigerant, and tools shown are mutually compatible.
5. The image communicates one stage only; remove distracting future-step tools.
6. A qualified technician approves the image or replaces it with a staged real
   photograph.
